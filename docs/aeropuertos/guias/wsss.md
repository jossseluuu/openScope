### About this facility
Singapore Approach (CAAS), Singapore

Singapore Approach controls the arrivals and departures of Singapore Changi, one of the busiest international airports in Asia and the hub of Singapore Airlines. Its traffic is almost entirely international, with a large share of wide-body aircraft.

### Airspace
The airspace is the SINGAPORE/JOHOR AIRSPACE COMPLEX, from 2000 ft to FL250, as published in ENR 2.1 (AIRAC 29 OCT 2026), with the CHANGI CTR (surface to 3000 ft) of AD 2 WSSS. **An aircraft that descends below 2000 ft outside the CTR leaves the airspace**, and an arrival that does so is lost. The transition altitude is 11000 ft; departures are initially cleared to 3000 ft.

The game starts with the arrivals of the first ten minutes already inside the airspace, each on a different STAR and at a different distance; new arrivals then appear on their STAR shortly before the airspace boundary, at least 90 seconds apart. Traffic is an estimate: about 18 arrivals and 25 departures an hour.

### Runway Configurations
Changi has three parallel runways of 4000 m: 02L/20R, 02C/20C and 02R/20L. Runway 02R/20L is not available for civil use.

- **North configuration:** arrivals land on 02L and departures use 02C.
- **South configuration:** arrivals land on 20R and departures use 20C.

openScope chooses the configuration from the wind. ILS approaches are available on 02L, 20R, 02C and 20C.

### Instrument Procedures
All SIDs and STARs are RNAV1 (GNSS).

- **SIDs:** one family per runway (`A` 02C, `B` 20C, `C` 02R, `D` 20L, `E` 02L, `F` 20R).
- **STARs:** `A` (runways 02) and `B` (runways 20). They continue past the initial approach fix to the downwind or base leg, at 3000-4000 ft, from where aircraft are cleared for the approach.

### Ground Operations
Taxi times are approximate: about 7 to 8 minutes to any runway.

### Terrain
The airport lies on the east coast of Singapore at an elevation of 22 ft. The terrain is flat; Bukit Timah, the highest hill of Singapore, reaches 538 ft.

### Airports of Interest

#### WSSS (Primary Airport)
Singapore Changi International Airport

Located 17 km east of the centre of Singapore at an elevation of 22 ft.

### Deviations from Reality
- The lower limit of the airspace complex varies from 2000 to 3500 ft; it is modelled as 2000 ft. The edge of the CTR along the Kuala Lumpur/Singapore FIR boundary is drawn as a straight line.
- The STAR `LELIB3B` is not modelled (one of its waypoints is not published in ENR 4.4).
- Seletar, Paya Lebar and the airports of Johor and Batam, and their traffic, are not modelled.
- Taxi times and the split of the traffic between routes and airlines are estimates.
